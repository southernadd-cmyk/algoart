A new collection. A new floor. Every day.

Each daily collection occupies its own floor. Step inside a gallery shaped by the golden ratio. Discover drawings on the walls and sculptures made from their lines, then take the archive lift to another day.

How to explore

Keyboard: W A S D to walk, arrow keys to look, Shift to walk faster, Artwork details appear when you pause nearby; L opens the lift calendar. Use Tab, Enter and Escape for buttons and panels. Mouse and touch controls are also available; on a phone, drag to look and use the arrows. VR is available on supported headsets and browsers.
