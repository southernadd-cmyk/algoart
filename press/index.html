GOLDEN RATIO / HUMAN MARKS

PRESS KIT

A drawing machine organised by mathematics, with marks that refuse to look mechanical.

ALGO/ART is an open-source, browser-based generative drawing project by Adam Clement. Eight composition systems explore the golden ratio through reproducible drawings and expressive material simulations.

Images and background for journalists, editors, galleries and creative-coding publications.

THE IDEA

How far can the golden ratio govern a composition while its marks remain visibly human?

ALGO/ART uses the golden ratio as a compositional proposal, not an inflexible stencil. Eight distinct drawing systems explore proportion, rhythm, direction and space. Their geometry is interpreted through marker, tissue and graphite, watercolour, oil paint and action painting.

Every drawing is made by seeded JavaScript algorithms, not a text-to-image AI model. A seed and the relevant settings allow a drawing to be revisited. Visitors can change parameters, inspect compositional overlays, compare results and export raster artwork or SVG paths.

CREATOR

Short biography

Adam Clement is a UK-based computing educator and creative coder. He created ALGO/ART to explore how mathematical composition can coexist with expressive, human-looking marks.

Longer biography

Adam Clement is a UK-based computing educator and creative coder working with interactive browser-based systems. His work examines the relationship between mathematical structure, deterministic algorithms and visual expression. ALGO/ART brings these interests together in a drawing machine that uses the golden ratio to guide composition while simulating the texture and irregularity of physical marks. Related experiments extend this approach into collage and sound-responsive animation.
