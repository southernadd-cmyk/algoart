# Virtual gallery architectural textures

This experimental gallery uses three **CC0 1.0** material assets from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/license). These are actual photographic texture maps, not art generated by the project.

| Surface | Poly Haven asset | Creator | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Walls / plinths | [Plastered Wall 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/plastered_wall_02) | Charlotte Baglioni | Pale plaster colour and normal relief, softened by a neutral colour treatment |
| Main gallery floors / ramps | [Concrete Floor](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor) | eye-candy.xyz | Light concrete colour and GL normal relief |
| Timber chamber | [Wood Floor](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_floor) | Poly Haven contributor(s), see asset page | Warm timber floor to vary the spatial rhythm |

**Licence:** CC0 1.0. Attribution is not required, but sources are documented for provenance. Direct URLs are in `architectural-materials.mjs`.

## Implementation

- Downloads come from Poly Haven's static `dl.polyhaven.org` asset host, **not the Poly Haven API**. No API key or request to `api.polyhaven.com` is needed at runtime.
- **1K JPG** colour maps and matching OpenGL normal maps are used. On mobile/reduced-motion devices the normal maps are omitted.
- Scanned colour is gently normalised to the exhibition palette on an in-memory canvas. It stays photographed architectural detail, not a noisy visible photo of a wall.
- UVs are based on **real dimensions in metres** so large rooms do not stretch a one-square texture over an entire surface.
- Textures are shared by all gallery rooms and loaded asynchronously. A lightweight neutral locally generated surface stays available even when external downloads fail or visitors are offline.
- Sculpture **plinths** share the plaster treatment. Sculpture **artwork** and its paper/paint masks are never modified by the architectural material system.
- Regular Three.js physically based materials provide realistic diffuse reflection and subtle normal detail; the sculpture render and live gallery publishing remain independent.

## Known limitations

The external 1K maps are requested from Poly Haven's static download host; if blocked by a network filter, the gallery intentionally renders fallback surfaces rather than going blank. The assets are not currently vendored into this repository. Desktop in-browser verification of the full rendered result and measured FPS remains necessary.
