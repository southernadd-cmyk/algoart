Can one number compose a drawing?

ALGO/ART is a deterministic drawing machine built around the golden ratio. It makes artwork in your browser using code, not an AI image generator. Choose a composition, change the seed and decide whether the mathematics helped.

How does ALGO/ART work?

Eight composition systems arrange geometry through proportions, directions, hierarchy and deliberate empty space. Each can be drawn with five materials that interpret the same underlying structure differently. Seeded randomness makes a particular configuration repeatable.

The golden ratio, φ ≈ 1.618, can influence division, scale and position. Its associated golden angle is approximately 137.5°. These are organising devices, not a claim that all decisions are mathematically inevitable: many visual rules are chosen and calibrated by hand.

Eight ways of composing

  • Orbital Studies
  • Golden Trajectories
  • Recursive Divisions
  • Radiant Systems
  • Connected Fields
  • Growth Systems
  • Constructed Forms
  • Automatic Marks

The systems include curved trajectories, rectangular subdivisions, radial structures, connected networks, branches, constructed forms and freer marks. They are composition methods, not generative AI models.

Five ways of making marks

  • Marker — wavering ink and pressure changes
  • Tissue & Graphite — transparent layered pigment and pencil-like texture
  • Watercolour — washes and drying edges
  • Oil Paint — painted masses and bristle-like contacts
  • Action Painting — gestural pours, pools and splashes

Try it yourself

Open the drawing editor, choose a system and material, then adjust parameters or the seed. Compare variants in 4-Up mode, use Reveal to inspect the proportional guides, or use Describe for drawing information. Export PNG, SVG or plotter SVG, or copy a link to the artwork.

The daily gallery archives actual ALGO/ART output with dated editions and links back to the relevant settings. Published images are not AI-made promotional mockups.

Common questions

What is ALGO/ART?

ALGO/ART is a free browser-based generative drawing machine. Its JavaScript code makes repeatable artwork using golden-ratio-inspired composition and material-specific rendering.

Does ALGO/ART use AI to create images?

No. The artworks are drawn procedurally by seeded JavaScript algorithms, not by a text-to-image or diffusion model.

How does the golden ratio influence the drawings?

The eight composition systems use relationships such as golden-section proportions, golden-angle directions and phi-scaled structures. Other choices, including spacing, hierarchy and visual weights, are artistically tuned.

Can I reproduce a drawing?

Yes. Share links and gallery records preserve the seed, settings and renderer version needed to revisit a composition. Historical material versions are retained where needed.

Can I download my drawings?

Yes. You can export PNG artwork, source-path SVG and plotter-oriented SVG. Plotter output has not been verified with every physical machine.
