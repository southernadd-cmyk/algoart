People, models and materials

PROPORTION PIECE brings generative drawings, sculptures and architecture together with the work of these model makers and open-source projects. The reception credits collection highlights the same sources. These credits describe materials and models used by the active live and test tower renderers, including optional fallbacks.

Licence restrictions and outstanding checks

This review distinguishes assets currently loaded from archived files still publicly hosted. Creator attribution is provided below, but a credit does not itself grant redistribution permission.

  1. Elevator GLB — permission to host the model file is unconfirmed (active, optional). Elevator — Game Ready by Big5shadow is documented as using the Sketchfab Standard licence. Sketchfab's licence terms prohibit making the model available as a standalone file; the original GLB is present in public live and test asset folders. Next action: obtain separate redistribution permission or withdraw the publicly hosted GLB and keep the gallery's procedural lift.
  2. HDRMAPS panorama — public hosting rights unconfirmed (archived, not displayed by the current tower). A JPG derived from the Jedlina-Zdrój HDRI remains in the public test assets. HDRMAPS terms restrict redistribution and posting the original images on a public server. Next action: confirm permission covering the hosted derivative or remove it from public hosting.
  3. Other sources — attribution needs verification. The current paving/stone material paths reference OpenGameArt pack files 028.jpg and 048.jpg without a recorded creator or original licence; the creator/licence for the newly adapted hanging room signs also requires independent confirmation. Next action: recover the original source records before describing these assets as fully cleared.

Scene models

  • Door by Maria Stashko. CC BY 4.0. Textures compressed, scale normalised, and the frame and two door leaves separated for animation.
  • [FREE] Pothos Potted Plant — Money Plant by AllQuad. CC BY 4.0. Textures compressed and leaf transparency adjusted for real-time rendering.
  • Set of Cardboard Boxes by NotAnotherApocalypticCo. CC BY 4.0. Textures compressed and individual boxes reused in a small reception stack.
  • Plants Scene Free! by Nicholas-3D. CC BY 4.0. Selected original plant, grass and rock meshes, with resized textures and unused scene objects removed, reused throughout the exterior planting.
  • Art Magazines on Old Cabinet (Scaniverse LIDAR) by JFN. CC BY 4.0. Unused UV sets removed, texture resized and recompressed, scale adjusted, and an inner backing added behind incomplete scan edges.
  • Paper debris by Sousinho. CC BY 4.0. Textures resized and recompressed; selected paper objects rescaled and arranged around reception.
  • Chalkboard_sign_V1 by clon6767. CC BY 4.0. One of the four original A-frame signs was isolated, original triangle geometry preserved with quantised coordinates, café graphics replaced by a PROPORTION PIECE curatorial label, and the frame scaled and placed at the entrance to the Translation Area.

Hospital hanging signage attributed in the source code to Sousinho, under CC BY 4.0. The source author/licence should be independently reconfirmed against the original model download.

Architecture and furnishings

Reception: Minimalistic Modern Office by dylanheyes. CC BY 4.0. Selected task chair, preparation table and wall lights; transforms baked and textures optimised.

Poly Haven: plaster, concrete and wood textures, architectural materials and cast-iron street lamps. CC0. The charcoal black brick is separately sourced from ambientCG, not Poly Haven.

Reception wallpaper (including the entrance wall): Decorative Wall 2 by M. Tolga Arslan / ShareTextures. CC0 as listed by the source. User-supplied 1K colour, normal and roughness maps compressed to WebP and tiled at physical scale.

Decorative Wall 2 remains on the wallpapered faces.

Interior wall plaster: plastered_wall_02 by Poly Haven, CC0. Used for upper gallery wall and plinth finishes in the live and test galleries.

OfficeCeiling001 by ambientCG. CC0. Maps resized for web use.

Twin Spot — Twin Surface Spotlight by Innerscene. CC0.

Exterior reception roof

Chinese roof. Low poly model. by KSmodels, CC BY 4.0. Original curved tile geometry retained, textures compressed, and two sections fitted over reception. Matching brick gables and eaves infill follow the roof profile; a ridge cap closes the join.

Reception floor tiles

PBR Floor Tiles Material by Aman Bajwa, CC BY 4.0. Colour, roughness and normal maps extracted and recompressed; scale and normal strength adjusted. Used on the live and test reception floors.

Gallery speakers

Ceiling Speaker by spy_dogg, CC BY 4.0. Transforms baked, grille mounted downward, texture resized and recompressed. A single record feeds the room and lift speakers and continues across floor changes.

Reception gramophone

Grammaphone by josephineburns, CC BY 4.0. Repackaged as GLB and scaled for display; original geometry and colours retained.

The record alternates Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart’s Piano Sonata No. 16 in C major, K. 545 (first movement) and Claude Debussy’s Reflets dans l’eau. John F. Putz examined golden-section claims about Mozart’s sonatas, finding considerable deviations; Roy Howat argues for golden-section proportions in Debussy’s music. These are interpretations, not settled facts.

Mozart: First movement of Piano Sonata No. 16, K. 545. Recording supplied by Musopen and archived at Wikimedia Commons. Musopen has released this recording for worldwide public-domain reuse. John F. Putz, “The Golden Section and the Piano Sonatas of Mozart”, Mathematics Magazine 68 (1995), 275–282.

Debussy: Giorgi Latso, recorded by Asuas on 13 May 2017. Recording source, CC BY-SA 4.0. Original unaltered recording, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Lift model

Elevator — Game Ready by Big5shadow. The bundled asset documentation identifies a Sketchfab Standard licence, not CC0 or CC BY. Its model is loaded as an optional lift replacement, but the public raw GLB is directly downloadable: redistribution permission remains unverified and a credit does not resolve that restriction. The fallback lift is generated in the gallery code.

Visitors

Quaternius character models. CC0. Meshes optimised and poses adjusted for the gallery.

Reception hanging sign

Pub Sign by johhny3D, CC BY 4.0. Original bracket, chains and sign geometry retained; face texture adapted with PROPORTION PIECE procedural graffiti lettering over a phi symbol. Rescaled and mounted outside reception.

Reception church window

Ancient Church Window by MWOgier, CC BY 4.0. Surrounding scan masonry trimmed, geometry simplified, transforms baked and textures recompressed. Repeated as a row of three real front-wall openings, visible on both sides, with pale jointed masonry borders and shared stone courses.
