What the selected material changes

The generator records the geometric strokes independently from the visible physical-material rendering. Texture, edge pooling and pigment flow cannot faithfully be demonstrated by merely restyling an SVG stroke.

Turning the drawing into a sculpture

The installed gallery sculpture pipeline traces the archived vector/pigment source into extruded shapes; for archived images where tracing fails, an explicitly labelled raster-derived relief fallback exists. This section explains the pipeline, not a claimed exact mesh for this example.

Designing the exhibition room

Gallery dimensions are metres, rather than artwork pixels. The actual date-and-artwork-seeded architecture planner determines the room graph; the sculpture planner reserves accessible corners and keeps circulation clear.

Where each stage comes from

The seed, φ and drawing engines

The renderer uses a deterministic seed and engine-specific methods. The golden ratio and golden angle are mathematical constants; many other parameters are artist-calibrated choices, not mathematical identities. Source: phi.js and generator.js.

The marker's individual passes

For each mark, the marker renderer applies seeded wobble, variable width, opacity, possible dashed dry ink and overdraw passes. These variations are captured as actual path coordinates and style values. Source: marker.js.

Three-dimensional forms and exhibition architecture

Traced shapes are extruded with depth and assigned material colours; the room plan is generated from seeded grid patterns, 12.25 m cell spacing, variable room dimensions and graph connections. Source: sculpture-render-traced.mjs, sculptures.mjs, and layout-v2.mjs.
