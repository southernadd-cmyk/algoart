phi architecture study

A standalone architecture experiment, now scaled to the exhibition: 18 artworks generate an approximately 31 × 19 metre golden footprint and four rooms. Golden cells can merge or become voids crossed by glass bridges. No live tower geometry has been replaced.

Physical study: blue-grey strips are glass walkways across open voids; dark lines are walls, openings are doorways, tan square is the lift datum and terracotta blocks are provisional sculpture footprints. Artwork allocation uses the dated archive identities and true portrait/landscape split; panels and sculpture plinths are placeholders for actual artwork textures and forms.
