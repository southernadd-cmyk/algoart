GOLDEN RATIO / HUMAN MARKS

PRESS KIT

An ongoing artwork built around the golden ratio.

PROPORTION PIECE (formerly ALGO/ART) is an ongoing, open-source, browser-based drawing artwork by Adam Clement. Eight composition systems explore the golden ratio through reproducible drawings and expressive material simulations.

Previously presented as ALGO/ART. Images and background for journalists, editors, galleries and creative-coding publications.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

PROPORTION PIECE asks how far the golden ratio can guide a composition while the marks still resemble something made by hand. Eight drawing systems use proportion, direction and spacing to arrange forms. Marker, tissue and graphite, watercolour, oil paint and action painting give those forms different surfaces and textures.

The artwork is drawn by seeded JavaScript algorithms in a browser, rather than an AI image model. A seed and the saved settings make each result repeatable. Visitors can alter the controls, examine the geometry and export images or SVG paths.

The How It Works page tells the longer story of the project.

PROJECT DETAILS

Created by Adam Clement, a UK-based computing educator and creative coder. PROPORTION PIECE is free to use and its source code is public. The editor contains eight composition systems, five materials, reproducible seeds, a daily gallery and PNG and SVG exports.

For technical background and development notes, see the source repository.

ABOUT ADAM

Adam Clement is a UK-based computing educator and creative coder. He works with browser-based experiments that bring programming, mathematics and drawing together. PROPORTION PIECE grew from an interest in what happens when exact rules are asked to make imperfect-looking marks.
