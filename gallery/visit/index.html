Every daily collection becomes its own building. Landscape studies hang on the walls; portrait studies become sculptures made from the actual drawn lines and the visible pigment boundaries of each material. Choose your route through the work.

Keyboard: W A S D to walk, arrow keys to look, Shift to walk faster, E to inspect artwork, L to choose a floor. Use Tab, Enter and Escape for buttons and panels. Mouse and touch controls are also available; on a phone, drag to look and use the arrows. VR is available on supported headsets and browsers.
