HOW IT WORKS

Eight composition systems organise shapes and marks through proportion, direction, hierarchy and deliberate empty space. Five drawing materials interpret the same structures differently. Seeds make each configuration repeatable.

φ ≈ 1.618. The associated golden angle is approximately 137.5°. Both help organise drawings; neither dictates every artistic decision. Spacing, hierarchy and other choices are also deliberately tuned.

QUESTIONS

WHAT IS ALGO/ART?

A free browser-based generative drawing machine built with seeded JavaScript and golden-ratio-inspired composition.

DOES IT USE AI TO CREATE IMAGES?

No. Drawings are generated procedurally, not by a diffusion or text-to-image model.

HOW DOES THE GOLDEN RATIO INFLUENCE IT?

Golden-section cuts, golden-angle directions and φ-scaled structures guide arrangements, alongside artistic calibration.

CAN I REPRODUCE A DRAWING?

Yes. Saved links and gallery records preserve seeds, settings and renderer versions. Historical materials retain version compatibility.

CAN I DOWNLOAD THE DRAWINGS?

Yes. PNG, SVG and plotter SVG exports are available. Physical plotter operation has not been verified with every machine.
