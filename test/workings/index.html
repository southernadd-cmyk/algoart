What the gold lines show

They mark golden-ratio divisions of the page, at about 38% and 62%. They help reveal the structure, but not every ink line follows them.

Why the pen goes over a line again

The artist-like finish comes from drawing the same path more than once, with small changes in position, thickness and ink strength.

From the page to the exhibition

In the virtual exhibition, some portrait drawings become sculptures. Their marks are traced, pushed out into solid shapes and placed on plinths. Landscape drawings hang on the walls.

Each day's rooms are planned from that day's date and drawings, so the exhibitions grow and change.
