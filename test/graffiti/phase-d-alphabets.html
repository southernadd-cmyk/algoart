Graffiti lettering, nine ways

Nine independently constructed A–Z alphabets, with matching φ symbols. Built from analysis of the 60 complete sheets in the Bombing Science collection, with separate anatomy and drawing rules for every family—not nine painted versions of one font.

Original interpretations, not traced or authenticated handwriting by any of the contributing artists. The tower still uses its existing graffiti until these are approved.

Complete alphabet

What you're looking at

Each capital is independently authored as a set of curves or solid cuts specific to its style family. The angular alphabets use filled blades and squared corners; the flowing alphabets use ink gestures; the calligraphic family uses a fixed-angle broad nib so its thick/thin contrast follows actual pen direction.

The lettering is context-sensitive in compatible families: joining strokes begin and end on actual measured letter curves. Turn them off to judge each alphabet's anatomy without the word-level connections. Each family also has a separately designed φ symbol.

We combined the former Kinetic sweeping study with Rotational loops because their completed words were not distinctive enough. Its original geometry remains archived in the research code. The Arrow-cross handstyle has been reconstructed from the reference sheets with stronger marker gestures.

The 60 reference sheets remain in the user-supplied research archive. No reference photographs are copied into this website. This is a Phase D prototype for visual review, not the gallery release or the backfilled tower atlas.
