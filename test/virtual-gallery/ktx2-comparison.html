For a genuinely cold network comparison on your Radeon: open this page in desktop Chrome or Edge, press F12, choose Network → Disable cache, and keep DevTools open while clicking “Run four cold-network starts”. The page verifies that the texture files were transferred in each run; any cached transfers are flagged rather than counted as cold. You may need to reload the page after enabling Disable cache.

Cold-network mode disables neither browser shader caches nor GPU caches; it is not equivalent to restarting Chrome with a fresh profile. Other network resources may also influence startup. For the full-gallery frame comparison use the regular button; cold mode measures startup only to avoid unnecessary loading on slow connections. Nothing is uploaded.
