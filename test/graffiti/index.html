The real writing behind the experiment

Looking at TAKI 183's tags suggested direct capitals and economical pen movements. Matt Weber's photograph of an early Futura tag suggested overlapping shapes and movement across a whole word. Chaz Bojórquez's Placa/Rollcall and his account of Cholo lettering suggested controlled capitals, angular counters and broad-nib contrast.

GraffSpace’s tag guide informed the word flow, repeatable writer habits, movement-led paint coverage and selective closing marks.

The construction families also draw on Street Fame’s style guide and Bombing Science’s 61-artist alphabet collection: letter anatomy, counter shape, overlap and word flow. These are original procedural studies, rather than reproductions of the illustrated alphabets.

These observations inform the original procedural hands. The golden-ratio construction belongs to this experiment; the sources document the graffiti references.
