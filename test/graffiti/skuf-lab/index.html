How the study works

Select a stroke and drag its large anchor dots or smaller Bézier handles. Use Draw new stroke to author another gesture. Reorder strokes and specify contact lifts, then replay timed motion. SVG exports only the reconstructed lines; JSON preserves editable paths and annotations. Timing is synthetic minimum-jerk movement, not captured SKUF motion. Work is isolated from the production gallery.
