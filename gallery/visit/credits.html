People, models and materials

PROPORTION PIECE brings generative drawings, sculptures and architecture together with the work of these model makers and open-source projects. The reception credits collection records the same sources.

Scene models

  • Door by Maria Stashko. CC BY 4.0. Textures compressed, scale normalised, and the frame and two door leaves separated for animation.
  • [FREE] Pothos Potted Plant — Money Plant by AllQuad. CC BY 4.0. Textures compressed and leaf transparency adjusted for real-time rendering.
  • Set of Cardboard Boxes by NotAnotherApocalypticCo. CC BY 4.0. Textures compressed and individual boxes reused in a small reception stack.
  • FREE >> Small succulent in planter by GrrHero. CC BY 4.0. Geometry simplified, textures resized and recompressed, and scale adjusted for the outdoor planting.
  • Vintage Framed World Map by My Name Is This. CC BY 4.0. Geometry centred for wall display, with scale and materials adjusted for gallery lighting. Original texture pixels retained.
  • Art Magazines on Old Cabinet (Scaniverse LIDAR) by JFN. CC BY 4.0. Unused UV sets removed, texture resized and recompressed, scale adjusted, and an inner backing added behind incomplete scan edges.
  • Paper debris by Sousinho. CC BY 4.0. Textures resized and recompressed; selected paper objects rescaled and arranged around reception.

Exterior reception metal roof

Just a metal roof for your project by Abandoned Scans, Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0). The original downloadable scan is adapted for the gallery: mesh simplified, texture resized, size and proportions adjusted, and mounted as the exterior reception entrance roof. Attribution and original source are retained.

Reception floor tiles

PBR Floor Tiles Material by Aman Bajwa, CC BY 4.0. Colour, roughness and normal maps extracted and recompressed; scale and normal strength adjusted. Used on the live and test reception floors.

Gallery speakers

Ceiling Speaker by spy_dogg, CC BY 4.0. Transforms baked, grille mounted downward, texture resized and recompressed. A single record feeds the room and lift speakers and continues across floor changes.

Reception gramophone

Grammaphone by josephineburns, CC BY 4.0. Repackaged as GLB and scaled for display; original geometry and colours retained.

The record alternates Béla Bartók’s Music for Strings, Percussion and Celesta and Claude Debussy’s Reflets dans l’eau. The selection accompanies Ernő Lendvai’s analysis of Bartók and Roy Howat’s analysis of Debussy’s musical proportions.

Bartók: SWR Sinfonieorchester Baden-Baden und Freiburg, conducted by László Somogyi, recorded 3–7 December 1957. Ducretet-Thomson 260 C 099, Gagnaux Collection reissue. Streamed from IMSLP’s EU recording collection, which identifies the recording as public domain in the EU and non-public-domain in the US. Audio is not hosted by this gallery.

Debussy: Giorgi Latso, recorded by Asuas on 13 May 2017. Recording source, CC BY-SA 4.0. Original recording streamed without alteration.
